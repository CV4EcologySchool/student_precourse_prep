## Fish images and annotations

The supplied fish sample was already used in previous CV4Ecology assignments.
The photographs come from the John E. Randall fish-image collection maintained
by the [Bishop Museum](http://pbs.bishopmuseum.org/images/JER/).

**Photo credit: Photographs by J.E. Randall, Bishop Museum, Honolulu.**

These photographs form part of the life's work of renowned ichthyologist J.E.
Randall and are provided with the kind permission of the Bishop Museum for
research and educational use. They remain third-party materials and are not
covered by any repository-wide software or content license. Contact the Bishop
Museum about uses outside research or education.

The matching CSV files contain landmark annotations created as part of the research project associated with this dataset. The
annotations are copyright Tel Aviv University and are available under CC BY
4.0.
